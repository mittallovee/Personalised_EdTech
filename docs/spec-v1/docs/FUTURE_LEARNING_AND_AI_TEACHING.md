# Later Phases: Courses, Classes and Personalized AI Teaching

Status: confirmed future direction on 18 October 2026. These capabilities remain outside the first question-bank release. No course, class, recording, learner profile or concept video has been created.

## Confirmed capabilities

- Faculty can create courses.
- Faculty can conduct live classes.
- Faculty can provide recorded sessions.
- AI can teach students, including concept videos generated for a student's personalized need and current level.

Generic prerecorded content alone does not satisfy personalized AI teaching. Interactive teaching modes (text/voice/avatar/etc.), generation triggers and reuse-versus-new-generation policy remain open.

## Personalization and educational meaning

Define need/level before implementation. Possible evidence includes student/faculty requests, reviewed diagnostics/attempts, concept progress and prerequisites. These are proposed signals, not an approved mastery formula. Class, question difficulty 1–10, Bloom level and mastery are distinct; do not infer a student's level merely from an AI tag or question difficulty. Missing/stale evidence needs an explicit outcome rather than an invented level.

Version the targeted concept, learning objective, learner context/evidence, language and requested depth/pace in a teaching plan. Personalization should materially adapt explanations, examples and prerequisites, rather than only insert a student's name. Correctness, equations/visual consistency, accessibility and learning usefulness need representative educational evaluation.

## Future workflow direction

Faculty organizes courses/lessons and live/recorded sessions. Authorized students access assigned/enrolled content. Explicit requests or later learning evidence identify a concept need; AI prepares a teaching plan and generates a traceable concept-video artifact when appropriate. Generation triggers, faculty review, student release, budgets and latency targets remain future product decisions.

Keep concept/content/model/prompt/media versions and intended learner context. Reusable teaching components may reduce cost but must preserve personalization without sharing private learner evidence. Supported languages/voices/video capabilities need provider verification when selected. No live-media SDK, model, streaming service or media host is chosen or tested.

Extend organization ownership, roles, media access, student-data lifecycle and audit to these modules. Define enrollment/session/media states, event meanings, retention, cost attribution and failure/recovery before implementation. Full platform admin includes supported future features; faculty/student grants need detailed contracts.
